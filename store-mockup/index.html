04 · Store Features

☕

Made to Order

Every cup brewed fresh. No batch brewing. Espresso, pour-over, and cold brew only. Small menu — done perfectly.

🏷️

Order Your Blend

Founding members can order their personal blend at the counter. Their number called, their cup made from their recipe.

📦

Bag Retail Wall

THE 100 blends available for purchase in-store. Visitors can order any founding member's blend as a 12oz bag to take home.

🔒

No Generic Menu

No "house blend." No pre-made options. Every drink starts with a choice from the wall or a standard origin — nothing unnamed.

🌱

Amazon-Sourced Only

All beans traceable to small family farms in the Amazon basin. Origin cards displayed next to each blend on the wall.

📱

Tap to Redeem

Founding members scan their member QR from the app to redeem their daily free coffee. One per day, no exceptions.

Design philosophy

The store isn't a café.
It's a gallery for 100 people.

Most coffee shops design for volume. Brewify's flagship designs for meaning. Every seat is intentional. Every blend on the wall is a real person. The space is small by choice — because when everyone can get in, it stops feeling like something worth being part of.
